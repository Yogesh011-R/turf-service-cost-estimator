Why the cheapest choices cost what they do

  • Sign in with Google/Apple — free, and no phone number is collected. A login code on WhatsApp is about ₹0.13 each. A login code by SMS is the expensive route: a one-time government registration of ₹5,900 (required to send any SMS in India) plus about ₹0.20 per code.
  • Customer pays to your UPI / QR and staff mark it received — no payment-service fee at all. A payment service with a free introductory period (such as Zaakpay) is free up to ₹5 lakh of takings or 90 days, then about 2% + tax. Cashfree is about 2.06% and Razorpay / PhonePe about 2.36% of the amount paid through the app.
  • Starter hosting uses free plans — fine to trial the app, but it can be slow to wake up and has usage limits. Basic hosting at about ₹800 a month is the sensible minimum for a real launch.
  • Maps — one venue stays within the free monthly allowance, or a free map provider can be used, so this is usually ₹0.
  • Skip SMS where you can — phone notifications and email cover the essentials for free.
  • Delay the Apple fee — launching on Android first saves about ₹8,500 a year until an iPhone app is worth it.
